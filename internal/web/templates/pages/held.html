Mail that did not come from anyone on the contact list waits here. Nothing bounces, nothing is deleted, and nothing reaches the device until you decide. There is no time limit — messages sit here until someone acts.

Delivering one letter without reopening the channel

Restore the contact, deliver the message, then remove the contact again. Three separate actions, each one announced in the inbox and written to the change log.

There is deliberately no quiet one-off delivery. A letter appearing on the device with nobody told how it got there is the thing this whole design refuses to do — including when the person doing it is you.
