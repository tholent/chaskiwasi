What removing a contact does, and what it cannot do

Removing someone stops new letters in both directions: their mail waits for review instead of reaching the device, and nothing can be sent to them.

It does not remove the letters they already sent. Those stay readable on the device and in the mailbox, and this system cannot take them back. If you are removing someone for a safety reason, that is the honest limit of what this can do — the letters live in a mailbox you control, and hiding them on the device would only make the device disagree with the record. You should know that now rather than in the middle of an argument.
